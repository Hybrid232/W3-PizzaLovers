PizzaDelivery

PizzyDelivery is a Phaser Game made for your Browser. It is a topdown open-world delivery game, where you need to deliver pizza as fast as possible to your customers. Made with seperal levels with seperal deliveries PizzaDelivery is staying funny over a certan time. With the help of Pizzy, our little pizza-mascot you will discover the open world of pizzatown.

This is a project of students for university. Here won't be big support for anyone but we would love to get some feedback. After end of this project at university there will not be much development due to time-issues anymore.
